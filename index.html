    Our community rules

    Keep the GC fun, safe and respectful for everyone.

    1. GC display picture: Kisi member ki permission ke baghair group ki display picture change nahi karni.
    2. GC theme: Group ka theme bina owner ki permission change nahi karna.
    3. Respect: Bila wajah gaali, personal attack ya harassment allowed nahi. Mazaaq tabhi jab dono comfortable hon.
    4. Fake / alternate account: Agar aap fake ya alternate account use kar rahe hain, owner/admin ko privately bata dein. Honest disclosure par sirf is wajah se kick nahi kiya jayega; safety concerns alag se review honge.
    5. Add / remove members: Admins kisi ko add ya remove karne se pehle owner ki permission zaroor lein.
    6. Spam / self-promotion: Jo member GC mein spam kare ya apne GC ka invite link bheje, usay foran remove kiya jaye. Agar 1 hour ke andar action nahi liya gaya, responsible admin se admin role wapas liya ja sakta hai; owner final review karega.
    7. Privacy: Kisi ki private chat, photo ya personal details permission ke baghair share na karein.
    8. Fair play: Games aur admin requests mein fake claims ya spam na karein. Owner ka final decision community ki safety ke liye hoga.

    Rules parhna sab ke liye zaroori hai 💗
